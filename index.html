Privacy Policy for Baby Sound Buttons

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Baby Sound Buttons is developed by Rohrforge and is designed for babies and young children. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using the app.

Information We Collect

Baby Sound Buttons does not require users to create an account and does not ask users to provide their name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone recordings, or camera data.

The app uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertising. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain technical information, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location
  • App interactions, such as app launches and taps
  • Diagnostic information related to app and SDK performance
  • Device or account-related identifiers supported by the SDK

Baby Sound Buttons does not request the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID).

Advertising

Baby Sound Buttons uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. The advertising configuration is intended for a child-directed app. Personalized advertising is disabled and the maximum ad content rating is configured as G.

Google Mobile Ads may process limited device, network, diagnostic, interaction, and ad-delivery information in order to provide advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention services.

Children's Privacy

Baby Sound Buttons is designed for young children. Rohrforge does not intentionally request personal information directly from children.

Advertising in the app is configured for child-directed treatment and is intended to comply with applicable Google Play Families requirements.

In-App Purchases

Baby Sound Buttons offers an optional one-time purchase to remove advertising. Purchases are processed by Google Play.

Rohrforge does not receive or store payment card information. The app receives purchase-status information from Google Play in order to determine whether advertising should be removed.

The app may store a local confirmation of the Remove Ads entitlement so that the ad-free experience can continue when the device is temporarily offline.

Local App Data

Baby Sound Buttons stores limited preferences locally on the device, such as whether the Parents tutorial has already been shown and certain app state needed for functionality.

This information is not transmitted to Rohrforge. Local app data can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Third-Party Services

The app uses services provided by Google, including:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads
  • Google Play Billing

These services may process information according to Google's own privacy policies and data retention practices.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Retention and Deletion

Rohrforge does not maintain user accounts or a remote user database for Baby Sound Buttons. Locally stored preferences remain on the device until the app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Information processed by Google services is subject to Google's own retention and deletion policies.

Security

Data transmitted by integrated Google services is transmitted using encrypted connections.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features, services, or data practices change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
